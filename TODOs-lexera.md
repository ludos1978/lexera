User reported "not any good yet" — pointer capture ALONE doesn't bridge separate WKWebView processes.
  - Stage 2 (commit 147142c6): per-webview pointer tracking — each receiver webview ([embeddedBoardBridge.js](lexera-kanban/src/shell/bridges/embeddedBoardBridge.js)) installs document-level pointermove / pointerup listeners on `hierarchy-entity-drag-start` and routes local (clientX, clientY) straight into `window.__lexeraExternalDnd.hover/drop`. On local pointerup the destination calls drop AND broadcasts `cross-view-drag-handled` so siblings tear down their trackers AND the source webview's hierarchy.js resets its activeDrag state (the source's own pointerup never fires for cross-WKWebView drops). Pinned by `crossViewDragPerWebviewTrackingContract.test.js` (8 cases).
